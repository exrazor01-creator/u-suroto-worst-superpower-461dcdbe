# Worst Superpower

**Entry:** "I can summon rain, but only directly above my own umbrella."

**Characters:** 59 (gate: 1–140, one line)

## Why it fits the brief

1. **Genuinely useless:** summoning rain directly above your own umbrella achieves exactly nothing — the umbrella already keeps you dry. The power's only effect lands where rain cannot hurt you.
2. **Funny in use:** picture the hero raising both arms to the sky while a storm hammers the canopy over their head, bone dry, exactly as they would be without any power at all.
3. **Unexpected:** not flight, invisibility, or talking to animals — a weather power defeated by its own accessory.
4. **Tight and specific:** the umbrella is the concrete detail that fixes the power in the world.
5. **Clear on one reading:** one sentence, no setup needed.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it.
